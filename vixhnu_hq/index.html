About me

Hey, I’m Vixhnu—a 15-year-old introvert who spends way too much time writing Python, playing games, and keeping to myself.

Don’t expect a formal, resume-style portfolio here. Vixhnu HQ is more like my personal digital basecamp—a corner of the internet where I drop small Python projects, random scripts, silly experiments, and whatever weird ideas I end up building just to see if they work. I don’t actually make games, I just love playing them, and I vibe-coded this whole site so I’d have a clean spot to put my stuff without making it overly serious.

Most days are a mix of figuring out Python logic, breaking code until it finally clicks, jumping into a game, or recharging my social battery in complete quiet. Because I’m pretty introverted in real life, having a place like this makes it way easier to share what I’m building and drop my gamertags and socials without having to do awkward small talk.

Click around, check out whatever small projects are currently up, grab my gamertags or social handles if you want, and take your time looking through the space.
